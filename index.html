🍕 DeliveryETA — Food Delivery Time Predictor

A 2nd-year B.Tech AI/ML project by Vikranth Reddy. A ridge regression model trained on 600 synthetic food delivery orders predicts the delivery time from distance, restaurant prep time, traffic level, order size, rider experience and peak hours. 5-fold CV: R² = 0.919, MAE = 2.41 min vs a mean-guess baseline MAE of 8.46 min (mean delivery time 47.9 min).

How it works: your six numbers are standardised (subtract the training mean, divide by the standard deviation), multiplied by the model's learned weights, and summed with a bias to give the delivery time in minutes; predictions are floored at 10 minutes. The exact same weights run in this page and in the Python training script (train.py), so the browser result matches the trained model.

Try it: a nearby 3 km order with 15 minutes of prep, clear roads, 2 items, an experienced rider, off-peak → about 23.9 minutes. A far 12 km order with 40 minutes of prep, jammed traffic, 8 items, a new rider, at peak hour → about 73.6 minutes. A mid-range 7 km order with 25 minutes of prep, moderate traffic, 4 items, off-peak → about 43.2 minutes.

Honest note: this model was trained on synthetic orders generated from simple delivery logic, so it is a classroom demo — real delivery times also depend on the exact route, weather, the restaurant's live queue and the rider's actual path, which six numbers can't capture.
